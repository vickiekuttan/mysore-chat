Privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Mysore chat is a small, invite-only chatroom run as a hobby. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps about you.

What we keep

  • Your Google sign-in. When you join with Google we receive your Google account's email address, name and a Google account ID. They are used only to sign you in.
  • Your screen name and profile settings (name colour, modem, status line).
  • What you post: messages, images, the groups you join, and friend requests.
  • Who invited you, recorded when you join through an invite link.

Who can see it

  • Other members see your screen name, profile settings and what you post in rooms you share. They never see your email address.
  • Friend chats are visible only to the two friends.
  • The chatroom's founder can see account records in the database, for running the chat and handling abuse.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone else.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, in its Mumbai (India) region. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

  • When Global chat and all groups together reach 1,000,000 words, every message in them is erased automatically.
  • Friend chats and your account stay until you ask for them to be deleted.

Deleting your data

To have your account and everything you've posted deleted, ask the founder, or open an issue at github.com/vickiekuttan/mysore-chat/issues (don't post personal details there; just ask to be contacted).

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
